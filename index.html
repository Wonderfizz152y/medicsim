Settings

Voice input

🎙 Always listening (default): the mic turns on when the call starts. Just talk; each thing you say is sent when you pause. The mic pauses itself while the patient or instructor is speaking. Tap 🎤 once to mute or unmute. The screen stays awake during a call. Push-to-talk: hold 🎤 (desktop: hold Space). Headphones work best so the mic doesn't pick up the phone speaker.

Offline downloads (one time, free)

Optional online LLM patient (advanced, paid API, not needed)

The built-in rule engine is the default. If you paste an OpenAI-compatible API key, patient dialogue comes from the LLM, with the scenario as the system prompt. Vitals, treatments, and scoring stay rule-based. The key is sent straight from your browser to the endpoint and is stored in this browser only if you tick "Remember".

🩺 Provider Impression

Select your primary Provider Impression before ending. It's graded in the debrief.

LA County Provider Impressions (Ref. 1200.3). Pick the single best primary impression for this patient.

What can I say?

Talk normally; these are examples, not exact commands. You can ask two things at once ("What year is it and where are we?"). If the patient says "Sorry, what?", the box under the chat shows exactly what the app heard.
